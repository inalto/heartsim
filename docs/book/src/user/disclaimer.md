# Medical disclaimer

heartsim is an **educational simulation**. It is not a medical device and must not be used for diagnosis, treatment,
monitoring or any other clinical decision.

* The heart, the torso and the conduction system are simplified models. Every rhythm is a teaching example built from
  textbook criteria, not a recording of a patient.
* Intervals, amplitudes and morphologies are realistic enough to practise recognition and measurement, but they are not
  validated against clinical data for any individual. Real ECGs are noisier and more varied.
* Exported files (PDF, PNG, CSV, WFDB) say "simulated" in their header. They contain no patient data, and they must not be
  presented as patient recordings.
* Importing a real recording for review only displays it. heartsim does not interpret it, and its measurements are no
  substitute for a qualified reader or a certified analysis program.
