# B36 — Designed mitral and aortic leaflets (and pulmonary cusps) in the 3-D heart and the echo
**Crates** `xtask` (bake), `heartsim-geometry`, `heartsim-echo`, `heartsim-app`, docs · **Model** Opus (Sonnet for the
research note, Haiku for the docs) · **Milestone** M12 (valves) · **Depends on** B31 (designed leaflets as cones,
papillary muscles), B35 (animated valve format, pose, carrier, render, echo `contains`).

## Goal
The mitral and aortic valves get leaflets in the 3-D heart, as the tricuspid valve has since 0.25.0. No openly licensed
measured model was found for them (search of 2026-11-26, below), so these leaflets are **designed**: generated by rule
from published dimensions, on this heart's own valve rings. They use the pipeline B35 built (the `HSAV` file, the pose by
`valve_openings`, the disc carrier, the layer, the echo's mesh test), so a measured valve can replace a designed one later
by replacing one file. The pulmonary valve is made by the same cusp generator, so that all four valves have leaflets.

## What was searched and why nothing measured is used (2026-11-26; re-check before starting)
* `github.com/alexkaiser/heart_valves`: **BSD-3-Clause**. MATLAB code (`valve_generator/`) that generates design-based
  mitral and aortic valve models for a fluid solver (Kaiser, McQueen and Peskin, arXiv 1902.00018). No ready meshes. It is
  a designed model too. It may be read for its method and its cited dimensions. If any of its code is translated, its
  copyright notice and licence text go into the repository's third-party notices; prefer writing the generator from the
  literature.
* FEBio Model Repository: an image-derived mitral valve of a 15-year-old with chordae (arXiv 2509.22865). Each project has
  its own licence, visible only after a login in FEBio Studio. **Not checked. Do not download it.** If the maintainer
  reports a usable licence (a non-commercial one counts, see the licence rule below), the mitral valve becomes a B35-style
  bake in a separate brief.
* `github.com/Ulrich-Unterberg/Aortic-Valve-Geometry`: **CC BY-NC 4.0**. Read on 2026-11-26 (readme, licence and notice
  only). Not used, for reasons other than the licence: it is not a measured valve but a CAD reconstruction of the
  parametric geometry of Haj-Ali et al. 2012 (*A general three-dimensional parametric geometry of the native aortic valve
  and root for biomechanical modeling*, J Biomech); it is one static shape with no open-to-shut sequence; and its
  `NOTICE.txt` says modifications need the copyright holders' permission, which a bake cannot meet. The useful part is
  the paper: read Haj-Ali 2012 as a dimension source for the cusp generator and cite it in the research note. Do not copy
  the repository's meshes.
* Zenodo 13236390: CC BY 4.0, static aortic valve surfaces from CT, derived from a challenge data set whose terms were not
  checked; no motion. Not used.
* The idealised tricuspid benchmark in `docs/research/tricuspid/` is not a mitral or aortic valve. Not used.

**Licence rule for this brief (maintainer, 2026-11-26).** heartsim is non-commercial, so a source under a non-commercial
licence (CC BY-NC, CC BY-NC-SA) may be used and may ship in any pack, the default one included (`CLAUDE.md`
non-negotiable 1). This brief still ships
no such asset, because the search found none that fits. If the re-check before starting finds a measured mitral or aortic
valve with an open-to-shut sequence under such a licence, stop and report it to the maintainer instead of baking it: it
needs its own brief, and its terms then bind everyone who receives the pack.
No-derivatives licences (ND) and terms that forbid modification stay unusable, since the bake modifies the mesh.

## Non-negotiables for this brief
* **Clean room.** Every dimension comes from a paper the implementer has read, with the page or table, or is marked
  **Designed** with the reason. Nothing from `epicardio/`, `epicardio-extracted/`, echosuite.app, any other simulator,
  any atlas image, or the meshes of the sources listed above as not used.
* **Honest.** The docs, the layer's hint and the pack `LICENSE` say these three valves are designed shapes, not anatomy
  of a person, and not reviewed by a clinician. The tricuspid valve stays described as one donor's valve. No lesion is
  implied: prolapse, stenosis, bicuspid valves and calcification are out of scope.
* **One source of truth.** Each valve's opening is the value `valve_openings` returns for it. No new clock, no new
  engine state. Engines, ECG and sim goldens untouched.
* **Deterministic and pure.** The generator is a pure function of (the pack's valve disc, the blood label volume, the
  designed parameters). No solver, no random numbers without a fixed seed, no I/O or GPU in `geometry` or `echo`.
* **The 3-D heart and the echo must not disagree** about a valve's shape or opening: both read the same posed mesh.
* **Performance is a gate** (see scope item 5): the M-mode trace is already slower than real time on the build server.

## Scope
1. **Research note** (Sonnet), `docs/research/designed-valve-leaflets.md`, before any code. One table per valve, each row
   with value, source and page, or "Designed":
   * Mitral: anterior and posterior leaflet lengths (already in `DesignedParams`: Tarkiainen), the share of the annulus
     each leaflet takes, commissure positions, the three posterior scallops and their indentation depth, coaptation
     length and depth, leaflet thickness, chordae: how many are drawn, where they insert, which papillary muscle serves
     which half of each leaflet.
   * Aortic: cusp geometric height and effective height (already in `DesignedParams`), free-edge length, commissure
     height, the nodule, cusp thickness, the opening shape in systole (orifice shape and area against the annulus).
   * Pulmonary: the same list; where no source is found, the aortic value marked Designed.
   * The orientation rules: which landmarks of this heart fix each leaflet's place (see item 3).
2. **Generator** (`heartsim-geometry`, pure, tested without the pack on a synthetic disc): from a `DiscRim`, a
   downstream direction and the parameters, build an `AnimatedValve`:
   * *Atrioventricular* (mitral): leaflets as a strip hung from the annulus, with per-leaflet height profile along the
     rim (anterior, posterior with three scallops, commissures), triangulated to about 2,000–3,000 triangles; chordae as
     segments from the free edge and the rough zone to the tips of the two designed papillary muscles the echo already
     has (`papillary_muscles`). Keyframes from open (leaflets along the inflow, toward the wall) to shut (coaptation
     line with the designed overlap, slight billow below the annulus plane, never above it).
   * *Semilunar* (aortic, pulmonary): three cusps, each attached along a crown-shaped line from commissure to commissure,
     free edges meeting at the centre at the effective height when shut; open, the cusps lie toward the vessel wall and
     leave a rounded-triangle orifice. No chordae.
   * Keyframes: K ≤ 8 shapes, `closure` strictly increasing, as B35 defines it. Between two keyframes no vertex may move
     more than 25 % of its whole open-to-shut path (no snap). Leaflets of one valve may touch but not cross by more than
     the drawn thickness at any opening.
3. **Fit and orientation** on the default pack, each rule stated and tested:
   * Mitral: the anterior leaflet faces the aortic valve (its centroid is the leaflet centroid nearest the aortic disc);
     the commissures are placed from the research note's rule.
   * Aortic: the cusp between the two that face the mitral valve and the atrial septum is the non-coronary cusp; the
     right coronary cusp faces the right ventricular outflow. If the pack has no coronary landmark, say so and name the
     landmarks used.
   * Pulmonary: named anterior, left and right by the same kind of rule.
   * Containment: no leaflet vertex more than 1 mm outside the valve's own blood (LV and LA; LV and aortic lumen; RV and
     pulmonary lumen) at any keyframe. The pack's vessels are tubes without sinuses: if the open cusps do not fit, shorten
     the opening, do not edit the vessel, and report it.
4. **Bake**: `cargo xtask bake-valve` gains the designed valves (`--valves tricuspid,mitral,aortic,pulmonary`, default
   all). It writes `valve_mitral.bin`, `valve_aortic.bin`, `valve_pulmonary.bin`, lists them in `manifest.ron`, and keeps
   every other file byte-identical, the tricuspid file included. The pack format stays 4. The pack `LICENSE` gains a
   section: designed by heartsim (MIT OR Apache-2.0), with the papers the dimensions come from.
5. **Echo**: `EchoAnatomy` holds a mesh valve for every valve the pack has; the cone of such a valve is no longer drawn
   in the labels, as for the tricuspid valve today. The cones stay for what reads them (centres, axes, view checks).
   * Semilunar cusps use `cusp_thickness_mm`, the atrioventricular leaflets `av_leaflet_thickness_mm`.
   * The *Mitral tips* cursor, the standard views' scores and the self-test must give the same answers as today within
     stated tolerances, or their targets are re-derived from the mesh and the change is listed.
   * **Gates** (criterion, release, same machine, before and after): a 128×512 slice through each valve costs at most
     10 % more than today; one M-mode column costs at most 5 % more; the grid rebuilds only for a valve whose opening
     changed. If a gate fails, keep the cone in the echo for that valve with an agreement test (B35 option b) and report.
6. **3-D heart and UI**: `HeartView` and `HeartMotion` handle a list of valves instead of the one tricuspid field.
   Layers *Mitral valve*, *Aortic valve*, *Pulmonary valve* beside *Tricuspid valve* in *Anatomy and camera* (en and it),
   each off by default, each replacing its flat disc and taking its closure flash when shown. A hint under the four
   toggles says which valve is measured and which are designed. Settings: the per-valve choices are saved; a settings
   file from 0.25.0 with `tricuspid_valve` still loads with its value.
7. **Docs** (Haiku): the user pages *The beating heart* and *The Echo page*; known-issues (designed shapes; flat rings and
   tube vessels under them; no sinuses; no clinician review; what was not seen on a real GPU or in a browser);
   `CHANGELOG.md`; `docs/ROADMAP.md` (M12); `ASSETS-LICENSES.md`; `docs/anatomy-pack.md`; one screenshot per valve if a
   screenshot can be made, otherwise say none was.

## Out of scope
Any lesion or variant (prolapse, flail, stenosis, bicuspid aortic valve, calcification, vegetations); the sinuses of
Valsalva or any change to the vessel or chamber meshes; coronary ostia; blood flow and Doppler; leaflet mechanics at run
time; changing valve timing, the sounds or the ECG; replacing the tricuspid valve; the FEBio mitral model.

## Acceptance tests (no GPU unless marked)
* Generator: same input, same bytes; on a synthetic circular disc the mitral leaflet lengths and the aortic effective and
  geometric heights equal the parameters within 0.5 mm; leaflet areas are reported; every triangle has a leaflet id; the
  annulus vertices lie on the rim within 0.1 mm.
* Keyframes: closures strictly increasing; opening 1 and 0 reproduce the first and last keyframes bit for bit; the
  free-edge distance to the coaptation point is monotone over 1,000 openings; the 25 % rule of item 2 holds; no crossing
  beyond the thickness at any of 1,000 openings.
* Shut valve: the largest gap between the free edges of the shut keyframe is below 1 mm for each valve (a competent
  valve); open valve: the orifice area is at least a stated fraction of the disc area, with the fraction's source.
* Fit: the orientation rules of item 3; containment within 1 mm, worst case and place reported.
* Motion: with the wall motion on, each annulus follows its deformed disc within 0.5 mm over a sinus beat; evaluations
  are bit-identical.
* Timing (sinus, complete AV block, atrial fibrillation): each mesh valve is shut exactly when its opening is 0 and open
  when it is 1; the mitral valve closes with M1 and the aortic with A2 (the events the sounds use) within one step; the
  aortic and mitral valves are never both open.
* Echo: for each valve, a label is leaflet if and only if the point is within the thickness of the posed mesh (20 poses,
  5 openings); the existing echo view, measure and self-test tests pass, with any changed target listed.
* Pack: `bake-valve` twice gives the same bytes; the tricuspid file and all other files are unchanged; a pack with only
  the tricuspid valve, and a format-3 pack, load and draw as in 0.25.0.
* App (egui_kittest): the four toggles exist in en and it, are off by default, survive a restart; a 0.25.0 settings file
  loads; each drawn valve's opening equals the echo's at the same instant.
* Render (GPU, lavapipe, skipped without it): each valve draws open and shut without validation errors.
* Bench: the gates of item 5; pose and render-fill cost per valve; bytes added to the pack and, deflated, to the web
  build.
* `cargo xtask ci`, both wasm32 checks and `cargo check -p heartsim-app --no-default-features --lib` pass; ECG and sim
  goldens unchanged.
