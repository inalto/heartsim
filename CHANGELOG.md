# Changelog

All notable changes to heartsim are documented here. Format: Keep a Changelog; versioning: SemVer.

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Six new patient cases (brief B44), nine in all: a regular wide-complex tachycardia, an anterior infarct with ventricular
  fibrillation and defibrillation, acquired long QT with torsades, atrial flutter with 2:1 conduction, a pacemaker that
  fails, and pericarditis against infarction (`assets/cases/case_*.ron`, English only). Where the simulator cannot show a step
  the text tells it. No clinician has reviewed them; the statements to check are listed in the known issues.
- `heartsim-cli validate-cases` also checks that the right answer is not in the same place in more than half of a case's
  questions (the three first cases are exempt). The patient cases are listed in the user book's *Tutorials* page.

### Changed
- At start-up the 3-D heart shows the four valve layers (*Tricuspid valve*, *Mitral valve*, *Aortic valve*, *Pulmonary valve*)
  and hides *Valve planes* and the torso (brief B37). Existing installations get the valve layers switched on once, when they
  first run this version; after that the stored choice is kept.

## [0.26.0] — 2026-11-26

### Added
- Designed mitral, aortic and pulmonary valves in the 3-D heart (brief B36): *Mitral valve*, *Aortic valve* and *Pulmonary valve*
  layers (Italian *Valvola mitrale*, *Valvola aortica*, *Valvola polmonare*) in *Anatomy and camera*, off by default and saved
  with the preferences. Each is generated by rule from published dimensions on this heart's valve rings, not taken from any model,
  and no clinician has reviewed it. When shown, it replaces that valve's flat disc; it opens and shuts on the valve schedule and
  follows the wall motion. The designed valves are heartsim's own (MIT OR Apache-2.0).
- Anatomy pack files `valve_mitral.bin`, `valve_aortic.bin` and `valve_pulmonary.bin` (`HSAV` format, pack format 4).
- `cargo xtask bake-valve` gains `--valves tricuspid,mitral,aortic,pulmonary` (default: all four).
- The research note `docs/research/designed-valve-leaflets.md`: every source and every designed value.
- User page section *The mitral, aortic and pulmonary valve layers* (in *The beating heart*), and known-issues entries for the
  designed valves and the pulmonary trunk.

### Changed
- The Echo page's mitral, aortic and pulmonary leaflets (image and cross-section) are now these meshes, 1.6 mm (mitral) and 1.0 mm
  (semilunar) thick, whether or not the 3-D layers are shown. A pack without the valve files still draws the designed cones. No
  existing echo measurement target changed. Measured cost of the slice and the M-mode column: about +2 % to +19 % against the cones
  from run to run on a noisy machine; the speed is not confirmed.
- The default anatomy pack continues the pulmonary trunk 20 mm beyond the dataset's end, as a designed straight tube, so the
  pulmonary cusps have room. The pulmonary artery lumen goes from 3.6 mL to 11.8 mL. The RV outflow echo view's stored pose moved
  slightly.
- Default anatomy pack files changed: `labels_heart.bin`, `labels_thorax.bin` and `mesh_pulmonary_artery.glb`. Pack format stays 4;
  `valve_tricuspid.bin` is byte-identical to 0.25.0.
- Preferences gain the three new valve layer switches. A 0.25.0 settings file still loads.
- The echo's lung edge in front of the heart (the parasternal notch) is measured from the heart proper: the pulmonary trunk and
  its pericardium above the heart's top are left out, so the suprasternal window is as it was.
- The asset rule: since 2026-11-26 CC BY-NC and CC BY-NC-SA assets may ship if marked non-commercial; none ships in this release.

### Fixed
- The plug that closed the pulmonary stub in the echo's heart label volume no longer cuts a sliver of pulmonary wall out of the
  aortic lumen (aortic lumen 26.2 mL to 26.4 mL).

## [0.25.0] — 2026-11-26

### Added
- A measured tricuspid valve in the 3-D heart (brief B35): a *Tricuspid valve* layer (Italian *Valvola tricuspide*) in *Anatomy
  and camera*, off by default and saved with the preferences. It shows three leaflets and their chordae from one donor valve, Texas
  TriValve 1.1 (CC BY 4.0, Mathur et al. 2022), fitted to the pack heart's tricuspid ring. When shown it replaces the flat
  tricuspid disc, and the tricuspid closure flash is on the leaflets. It follows the wall motion and opens and shuts on the valve
  schedule. It is one donor's valve on another heart, for education only, and no clinician has reviewed it.
- Anatomy pack format 4 (brief B35): optional `valve_<name>.bin` files in the `HSAV` version 1 format, listed in the manifest's
  `valves`. The default pack gains `valve_tricuspid.bin` (155,832 bytes; 2,012 vertices, 3,000 leaflet triangles, 988 chordae
  segments, 7 keyframes). Format 3 packs still load and draw as before, without a valve.
- `cargo xtask bake-valve` adds a valve to an existing pack and leaves every other pack file byte-identical. `cargo xtask
  bake-anatomy` now bakes the valve too; `--no-valve` skips it.
- A credits line in the About window naming the three open data sources.
- The research note `docs/research/tricuspid-valve-source.md`: the source, its commit, the licence check, the rejected candidates
  and the fit numbers.
- User page section *The tricuspid valve layer* (in *The beating heart*), and known-issues entries for the valve.

### Changed
- The Echo page's tricuspid leaflets (image and cross-section) are the measured mesh, 1.6 mm thick, when the pack has the valve,
  whether or not the 3-D layer is shown. The mitral, aortic and pulmonary valves keep the designed cones. Measured cost of the
  slice: +3.7 % (8.32 ms against 8.02 ms on the development machine).
- `xtask` gains `lzma-rust2` 0.23 (Apache-2.0, pure Rust) to read the donor's LZMA-compressed frames. It is an xtask-only
  dependency; no shipped crate gains one.

### Fixed
- The xtask MD5 gave wrong digests for inputs whose length modulo 64 is 56 to 63. This affected only the bake's checksum check.

## [0.24.0] — 2026-11-25

### Added
- The Echo page (brief B31, echo simulator phase 2), in the Explore group after Heart Vector. A transthoracic probe is put on
  the figure from four windows (parasternal, apical, subcostal, suprasternal), then slid, tilted, rocked and rotated by drag,
  handles, sliders or keys; depth and sector are adjustable. The sector is drawn in the 3-D heart, with an optional cut at the
  image plane. A coloured anatomical cross-section, not an ultrasound image, shows the tissues along the plane with a legend, a
  depth scale in centimetres, a hover read-out and "No contact" where the beam misses the skin. Hidden areas behind rib or lung
  are dimmed and hatched. Valve leaflets, papillary muscles, the moderator band, the pericardium and the lung-edge retraction
  are designed and labelled as such.
- Anatomy pack format 3: two label volumes, `labels_heart.bin` (1 mm, the heart) and `labels_thorax.bin` (2 mm, the thorax), with
  the tissue labels in `docs/anatomy-pack.md`. A version-2 pack is refused with a message to re-bake it.
- User page *The Echo page: probe and cross-section*, and a known-issues entry.
- The grey simulated image on the Echo page (brief B32, echo simulator phase 3). The right panel shows a grey sector by default,
  with a display switch: *Image*, *Anatomy* (the B31 cross-section) and *Both* (the colours over the grey image, with the legend).
  The image comes from a simple line model: two-way attenuation, reflection and transmission at tissue boundaries, incidence
  weighting, bone shadow, lung reflection with designed reverberation, enhancement behind blood, speckle that moves with the
  tissue, a point-spread blur, time-gain compensation and log compression. It is labelled as a simulated image from a simple
  model, not a recording, and nothing was compared with a real echocardiogram.
- Image settings: frequency (2–5 MHz), gain (±30 dB), dynamic range (30–90 dB), focus (with an F marker on the depth scale), an
  automatic time-gain compensation with eight sliders, and a reset that also restores depth and sector (English and Italian).
- The acoustic table `assets/echo/acoustic.ron` (format version 1): one row per tissue, with the impedance, attenuation,
  backscatter, scatterer density, mean, spread and specularity, each marked read, unverified or designed.
- User page *The Echo page: probe, cross-section and simulated image*, with a *Reading the image* section; known-issues entries for
  the image; a developer note in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` (section 4.6).
- The standard views on the Echo page (brief B33, echo simulator phase 4), in a *Standard views* card with two modes. *Practise*
  asks for a view and scores the probe from 0 to 100 %, gives one hint in words, ticks a checklist of what the view must show,
  animates the probe to the reference pose with *Show me* (instant with Reduced motion), and can show a ghost of the target plane
  in the 3-D heart and name the structures on the image. A view is found at a score of 80 % held for one second. *Self-test*
  asks the shipped views without hints, ghost, checklist or labels, times each one and shows a summary; it has no pass mark, and
  its progress is saved like the other quizzes.
- Thirteen of the fifteen standard views are shipped, with their pages in Mitchell et al. 2019 (doi:10.1016/j.echo.2018.06.004).
  The targets are computed from the pack's landmarks. Two views are not reachable in this model: the subcostal inferior vena cava
  (the pack's vein is too short) and the suprasternal arch (best score 35 %). Tolerances, the marker rules the guideline does not
  give, and the hint wording are designed and not reviewed by a sonographer; the user page *Standard views* lists them.
- `heartsim-cli echo views` prints each view's status, score, hidden share, reference pose and guideline pages.
- Tutorial *Echo: the standard views* (16 pages, English): windows and probe movements, then the views in guideline order, each
  page with a "view found" check (the new `view_check` page field). The tutorial actions `ProbeAtWindow`, `ProbeAtView` and
  `SetEchoView` and the Echo panel are new.
- User page *Standard views: find the view with the probe*, a known-issues subsection on the standard views, and four screenshots
  (three views, and the self-test, which is scripted: its scores and times are not real).
- *Settings → Faster M-mode trace (echo)*, off by default: the M-mode trace takes a column every 50 ms instead of every 25 ms, at half
  the cost, for computers that cannot keep the full trace in real time. The columns are the full trace's own; a time read from the
  trace is then good to 50 ms, and the M-mode panel says so. A caliper reference or a snap on a column that was not taken now reads
  the column the trace draws there.
- M-mode, tissue Doppler and calipers on the Echo page (brief B34, echo simulator phase 5), in a fourth left-column section, *Measure*,
  with three modes: *2-D*, *M-mode* and *Tissue Doppler*. M-mode puts a cursor line on the sector and draws a depth-against-time trace of
  that scan line, one column every 25 ms of simulated time, with the ECG lead on the same clock, at 25, 50 or 100 mm/s; it can be frozen
  and scrubbed. Tissue Doppler places a gate on the septal or lateral mitral annulus or the lateral tricuspid annulus and draws its
  velocity along the beam, positive toward the probe, with S', e' and a' marked. The angle to the motion is shown and not corrected, and
  a thin band round the curve is labelled designed. There is no blood Doppler. Calipers measure distances, areas, depths, times, slopes
  and velocities. Up to four named measurements (LVIDd, LVIDs, IVSd, LVPWd, LA diameter, aortic root, TAPSE, MAPSE, e', a', S') show the
  model's own value for the same place and instant, with the difference. A named measurement can be sent to the matching echo
  calculator input, marked "from the echo simulator"; nothing measured is saved.
- User page *Measuring on the echo*, with four screenshots; known-issues entries for the B34 limits and the three open decisions; three
  measuring pages appended to the tutorial *Echo: the standard views* (19 pages now, English) and four tour steps. The conventions are
  cited from Mitchell et al. 2019 with their pages.

### Changed
- Buttons have more padding (8 × 4 points) across the app, and the top-bar group menus and their rows 12 × 6 points; the rhythm
  bar wraps at narrow widths and its picker has a fixed width (a long name is cut short, the full name on hover).
- Interface pass: one accent-coloured primary button per panel (Run, Check answer, Next question, Play, Start or Continue); shared
  slider rows, helper text at a readable size and contrast, "More" for long explanations, tick rows, segmented controls and
  collapsible sections. Selected tabs and pills have at least 4.5:1 text contrast in both themes and in high contrast.
- Simulator: the left panel is five collapsible sections (Colour map, Click on the heart, Anatomy and camera, Wall motion, Heart
  vector; the first three open at first, the state remembered) in a scroll area; layers are listed by their translated names; the
  ablation hint reads "Shift-click the heart to ablate without switching mode."
- Narrow windows (below 700 px): the Echo page shows an *Image | 3-D chest* switch above the figure and keeps the sector (and the trace
  when M-mode or Tissue Doppler is on) at a fixed height, with the section control and its controls scrolling below. The Echo page's
  left column is 300 px wide by default.
- Echo page: the left column is one section at a time (Probe | Image | Views | Measure); the image keeps its rectangle in Image, Anatomy and
  Both, with the read-out and legend below it; the description is one line with "More"; Reset has its own row; the drag hint goes
  after the first probe move; the checklist shows ticks; the self-test table has Best and Time columns ("—" for a view not found);
  the view order follows the guideline (RV outflow before RV inflow, RV-focused four-chamber after the apical four-chamber).
- Narrow windows (below 700 px): Simulator, 12-lead, Sound Trainer, Auscultation Quiz, EP Lab and Electrodes show one part at a
  time behind a segmented control; the interval drill, rhythm quiz, axis quiz, axis trainer and heart vector stack the trace above
  the controls; the ECG strip toolbar folds into a lead picker and a Display menu.
- Interval drill: a line says what to do next while Check answer is disabled. Calculators: a legend explains the italic,
  formula-only entries, and the disclaimer is larger. Sound trainer: a legend explains the bracketed listen count.
- Tutorials and cases lists: one accent button on the page (Continue on the last opened unfinished tutorial, otherwise Start on the
  first not completed).

### Fixed
- Starting the echo self-test prepares the views one per frame with a "Preparing views n of 15" progress bar, instead of blocking
  the page for the whole preparation.
- The 12-lead beat markers no longer overlap the lead labels.

## [0.23.0] — 2026-11-23

### Added
- The beating heart: wall motion of the 3-D heart (brief B30, echo simulator phase 1). Each region contracts after its own
  activation for about the length of its action potential; the valve closures that place the heart sounds also bound ejection
  and filling, so the motion, the ECG, the colour map and the sounds agree. Sinus beats show the base descending toward the
  apex, thickening walls and left-ventricular twist; LBBB shows a small septal flash; a premature beat does not eject and the
  beat after it does; AF has no atrial kick and a beat-to-beat ejection fraction; complete AV block has atria on their own
  clock; an ischaemic territory moves less; VF and asystole show no organised motion.
- New pure crate `heartsim-echo` (depends on core, geometry, sim and ecg; no GPU, no I/O, builds for wasm32): the kinematic model
  in cardiac coordinates, the phase gating, the beat-to-beat ejection fraction and the mapping to every mesh of the pack. It is a
  model of wall motion, not electromechanics: no pressure, no flow, no Doppler. Every parameter is in one file with its source or
  the word "designed".
- Heart panel controls: a **Wall motion** switch (on by default, off while Reduced motion is on, remembered), an
  **Exaggeration** slider (0.5× to 1.5×, 1× by default; the read-outs always use 1×) and a **Wall-motion read-out** card with
  LVEF, LV end-diastolic and end-systolic volumes, MAPSE and TAPSE, each labelled *model* (English and Italian).
- User page *The beating heart (wall motion)*, a known-issues entry, and the layering line with `heartsim-echo` in the architecture
  docs.

## [0.22.0] — 2026-11-22

### Added
- Echo calculators (brief B29, part A): a fourth group in the top bar, **Tools** (it: *Strumenti*), with the page **Echo
  calculators** (*Calcolatori eco*). In part A, 58 calculators in nine categories (stenosis, regurgitation, filling pressures, left
  ventricle and aorta, right heart and pulmonary circulation, pericardium, shunts and congenital, prosthetic valves, ultrasound
  physics), each with its inputs in order of acquisition, units and plausible ranges, the live result, the formula with the
  numbers substituted, the findings with the criterion they meet, the reference with table and page and a DOI link, a worked
  example and a review badge: 30 are **verified** (formula and every threshold read in the primary guideline or paper during
  this work) and 28 **formula only** (standard arithmetic, no grading). Search by name or measurement in English and Italian,
  favourites and the last calculator remembered (typed values never stored), Tab and Enter between fields, decimal comma in
  Italian, a phone layout, and the heart rate of the running rhythm offered to the cardiac-output and shunt-flow calculators.
  The educational disclaimer is on the page; findings are worded as criteria, never as diagnoses.
- New pure crate `heartsim-calc` (no dependencies, no I/O, builds for wasm32): the calculator model (`Calculator`, typed
  `Inputs`, `Outcome` with values, worked steps and `Finding`s carrying criterion and locator, graded tables as data), the
  shared building blocks (Bernoulli, areas, stroke volume, continuity, PISA, body surface area, indexing) and the registry.
  Property tests check that no in-range input gives NaN or infinity.
- Echo calculators, part B (brief B29): 16 guideline decision tools, each a decision tree whose every step and result cites
  the figure or table and page: diastolic function and LAP in sinus rhythm (ASE 2025, Figure 3, with the age-specific e' of
  Table 6) and in atrial fibrillation (Figure 8), moderate or severe MAC (Figure 4), pulmonary hypertension (Figure 6), heart
  transplant (Figure 5), diastolic exercise echocardiography, and the Table 7 indicators for mitral stenosis, mitral
  regurgitation, HCM, restrictive cardiomyopathy, sinus tachycardia and LVAD (with the LAP from the RAP and septal position);
  aortic stenosis stepwise grading with the low-flow, low-gradient pathway, dobutamine and calcium score (EACVI/ASE 2017,
  Figure 8); aortic prosthesis screening (ASE 2024 Figure 13 and Table 5) with mismatch (Table 7), the serial comparison of an
  aortic bioprosthesis with its baseline (Tables 5 and 6), and mitral prosthesis screening (Tables 11 and 12). The page shows
  the path taken; any field may be left empty, and the result is **indeterminate** (with the results still possible) only when
  an empty field could change it. Two new finding classes, *abnormal* and *indeterminate*.
- Sex-specific normal ranges and abnormality partitions (chamber quantification 2015, Table 4 and Supplemental Table 3) for
  the ejection fraction, the LV diameters and volumes (absolute and indexed) and the linear LV mass, wall thickness and
  geometry: an optional *Sex* input; *ejection fraction*, *indexed LV diameters* and *indexed LV volumes* are now verified.
  74 calculators, 49 verified.
- `heartsim-cli calc list` and `heartsim-cli calc <id> input=value … [--example] [--json] [--lang it]`.
- Docs: user page *Echo calculators*, developer page *Adding a calculator*, the source table `docs/research/echo-calculators.md`.

### Changed
- With fourteen pages the top bar shows every page as a tab from about 1380 px (English) instead of 1280 px; narrower windows
  show the four group menus.

## [0.20.0] — 2026-11-21

### Added
- Heart sounds, phase 5: teaching (brief B28). **Sound Trainer** (Practise): 22 single findings (S1 and S2, the four splits,
  soft and variable S1, S3, S4, summation gallop, clicks, opening snap, each murmur, rub) as loops cut at a quiet point of
  diastole into whole cycles (irregular rhythms: 9 s with fades), with Play/Stop, a half-speed version (labelled as altered
  sound), the trace shown or hidden, site and chest piece, breathing, and a listen counter per finding with your total, kept
  between sessions. Four contrast pairs (innocent murmur / aortic stenosis; ejection / regurgitant murmur; split S2 / opening
  snap / S3; S4 / split S1) switch with one key (S) at the same point of the cardiac cycle. **Auscultation Quiz** (Practise):
  a hidden auscultation case loops with the trace and name hidden; tick the features you hear, then choose one of four
  diagnoses; feedback shows the trace with its labels, the ECG, which features were right and the teaching text. Features and
  diagnoses are scored separately and kept; slow motion only in the feedback step. Without a sound output both pages show the
  trace and say why.
- Tutorials `11_heart_sounds` (sound check with a 200 Hz and an 800 Hz tone, S1/S2 against the ECG, the split and breathing,
  slow motion, long PR, complete block, LBBB, atrial fibrillation, S3, S4) and `12_murmurs` (systolic and diastolic murmurs,
  aortic stenosis, the innocent murmur, aortic regurgitation, mitral stenosis, where to listen). New tutorial actions:
  `HeartSoundAudio`, `HeartSoundLane`, `Listen(site, chest)`, `SoundCheck`, and the panels `SoundTrainer` and
  `AuscultationQuiz`. `heartsim-cli run-tutorial` runs them headless (audio actions are no-ops there).
- Breath-hold: a scenario-timeline action `BreathHold(phase: Some(EndExpiration | PeakInspiration))` (`None` breathes again)
  and a Breathing choice in the Display popup. The respiratory clock of the timing engine freezes, so the sinus arrhythmia and
  the S2 split stop following the breath; scenarios without the action are bit-identical.
- Small-speaker mode (Settings → Heart sounds, off by default): every heart-sound frequency ×3 in what is played (designed),
  so S3, S4 and the rumble reach laptop and phone speakers; the lane keeps the true trace and is tagged "altered sound".
- WAV export of the heart sounds (Export window, and "Save WAV" in the Sound Trainer): 16-bit mono at 44.1 kHz through the
  sound output's resampler, with the scenario, the listening site and "synthesised by heartsim" in the INFO chunk. Desktop only.
- `docs/research/heart-sound-clips.md`: candidate real recordings (CC0, public domain, CC BY) with their licences as read on
  the primary pages, for the maintainer's approval. No recording ships yet.

### Changed
- The band-limited resampler moved from the app to `heartsim_ecg::resample` (shared with the WAV export); the app re-exports it.
- The navigation has thirteen pages; the new two have no number-key shortcut.

## [0.19.0] — 2026-11-21

### Added
- Heart sounds, phase 4 part A (gallops and murmurs, brief B27): an optional `auscultation` section in the scenario files
  (`s3`, `s4`, `rub`, `fixed_split`, `lesions: [(kind, severity)]`; no format bump, files without it sound exactly as
  before). New sounds in the phonocardiogram, scheduled from the same engine events as the ECG: S3, S4 (from the P waves:
  never in atrial fibrillation, in complete block it follows the P waves), the summation gallop, aortic and pulmonary
  ejection clicks, the mid-systolic click of prolapse, the opening snap, a three-part pericardial rub, and murmurs for aortic
  and pulmonary stenosis, aortic, mitral and tricuspid regurgitation, mitral stenosis (with presystolic accentuation in sinus
  rhythm only), mitral valve prolapse, ventricular and atrial septal defect (fixed split) and the innocent murmur. Ejection
  murmurs grow after a long cycle, mitral regurgitation does not; tricuspid regurgitation grows on inspiration. The lane labels
  `S3`, `S4`, `OS`, `EC` and `C`; murmurs show as their envelope. Murmurs are seeded sums of sinusoids, so the sound stays
  deterministic and random-access, and keeps its pitch in slow motion. All spectral values are designed: the sounds are a
  model, not recordings.
- 15 heart-sound scenarios with an ordinary ECG (S3 in heart failure, S4 in hypertrophy, summation gallop, aortic stenosis
  mild and severe, aortic and mitral regurgitation, mitral stenosis in sinus rhythm and in AF, prolapse, tricuspid
  regurgitation, pulmonary stenosis, VSD, ASD, innocent murmur), under their own heading in the rhythm list and never drawn by
  the Rhythm Quiz or the Interval Drill. Acute pericarditis now plays a friction rub and the anterior STEMI an S4.
  `ExpectedFindings` gains `extra_sounds`, `murmurs` and `s2_split`.
- Heart sounds, phase 4 part B (listening sites, brief B27): a *Listening site* control in the *Display* popup with the
  overview (the default, today's mix bit for bit) and the aortic, pulmonic, Erb's point, tricuspid and mitral (apex) areas,
  also as clickable markers on a chest drawing placed from the electrode editor's intercostal landmarks; *bell* or
  *diaphragm* at a site. A site scales each heart sound and murmur by a gain from `assets/auscultation_sites.ron` (designed,
  following only the textbook statements: S1 loudest at the apex, A2 dominant at the aortic area, P2 at the pulmonic area,
  each murmur loudest over its valve, aortic regurgitation at Erb's point); the chest pieces weight every murmur partial and
  the instantaneous frequency of every discrete sound (no filter state: still deterministic and random-access). The bell
  brings out S3, S4 and the mitral rumble, the diaphragm suppresses them. The lane, its title (*Heart sounds (model) · Apex
  · Bell*) and the sound always follow the site; the choice is remembered with the settings.
- Valve highlights: with the *Valve planes* layer visible, each valve disc of the 3-D heart flashes at its closure (M1, T1,
  A2, P2) and a stenotic or regurgitant valve glows for the length of its murmur, from the same schedule as the sound and
  with the same display delay. *Reduced motion* turns them off.
- User guide: *Extra heart sounds and murmurs*, with the listening sites, the bell and the diaphragm and a chest figure.

## [0.18.0] — 2026-11-21
Seventeenth tagged release: the heart sounds also play in the browser build, after a click of the learner.
Not yet listened to in a real browser; latency figures are unmeasured.

### Added
- Heart sounds, phase 3 (browser, brief B26): the web build plays the heart sounds through Web Audio with the same player,
  samples and controls as the desktop (switch, volume, audio offset, sync check, slow motion, muting above 1×, pause, *Play*
  from the cursor with the moving playhead). Chunks of the phonocardiogram are resampled to the context rate and scheduled on
  the audio clock 100 ms ahead (contiguous to the sample, merged to at least 20 ms, re-anchored with a fade-in after a gap,
  drift-corrected); the views are drawn the look-ahead, the 40 ms queue and the browser's reported latency behind. Sound
  starts on the learner's first click or key press; until then the *Display* popup, the Settings window and the heart-sound
  lane say *Click anywhere to enable sound*. Hidden tabs suspend the audio context; on return playback restarts from the
  present. `?sound=1` in the URL switches the sound on at start. `window.heartsimStatus.audio` reports the state, the sample
  rate, the chunks scheduled, the queue and the latency. Still a plain static site: no threads, no AudioWorklet, no headers.

### Changed
- The heart-sound output is chosen at run time: the embedding crate registers a sink factory (`HeartSoundPlayer::set_factory`,
  `HeartSimApp::set_heart_sound_output`), and the sound controls appear wherever there is one. `PcmSink` reports its own
  constant delay (`latency_ms`, 40 ms for kira as before) and whether it can play now (`readiness`).
- In the browser the *Audio offset* defaults to 0 ms (desktop: 60 ms, unchanged).

- The atria, caval veins, coronary sinus and pulmonary veins follow the thorax too: the veins now enter the left atrium at or above the
  mid-ventricular level instead of beside the apex, the SA node sits at the SVC–RA junction, the P axis is about +33°, and the AV node
  and His bundle stay at the septal crest. A new landmark, `la_pv_antrum`, marks the posterior left atrium between the veins.
- The persistent-AF presets (`af_persistent`, `ep_af_pvi`) are re-tuned for the new atria: the burst starts at `la_pv_antrum` at 125 ms,
  the atrial refractory period varies from patch to patch, and sensing (`sense_window_ms` 400, `sense_gap_ms` 135) and AV concealment
  (0.85) are set for conduction at 0.3 ×. The typical-flutter preset's circuit moves with the right atrium.
- Tab shortcuts: `0` opens the tenth tab (EP Lab); the new Interval Drill tab sits before Tutorials, so Tutorials is now `9`.
